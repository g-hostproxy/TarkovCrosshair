# Changelog

## v1.1
- **TarkovSway recalibrated against an in-game laser.** About 235 ms response delay, 39 px strafe lean and a smoother swing: the crosshair now misses the laser by ~11 px on average, down from ~28 px.
- **Forward dip / backward rise:** walking forward drops the crosshair (22 px), walking back lifts it (12 px).
- **Recoil tab:** visual climb on fire (semi / full auto, fire rate, vertical and horizontal kick, max climb, recovery). The mouse is never moved.
- **Dynamic spread:** the gap opens while you move and fire.
- **Presets:** locked, laser-calibrated Tarkov Default plus 3 auto-saving slots.
- New sliders: response delay, wiggle damping, forward dip, backward rise.
- Jump kick, optional hide-in-inventory (Tab), hide while sprinting.
- The crosshair is no longer tied to the game window: it stays on screen until you hide it.
- UI: taller window, bigger preview, tabs sized to their labels, no cut-off text.

## v1.0
- First release: 8 shapes, colour/size/gap/opacity/outline, hide-when-aiming, TarkovSway, tray icon, global hotkey.
